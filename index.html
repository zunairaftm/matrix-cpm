How to read the result

A higher total weighted score means a stronger competitive position relative to the other firms, as long as all three are rated on the same critical success factors and weights.

In the CPM, 2.5 is the midpoint of the 1 to 4 scale, but unlike the EFE Matrix it is not a threshold for calling a firm strong or weak. A firm that ranks last is not necessarily weak in absolute terms, so also review the ratings on individual critical success factors.
